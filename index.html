I am a final-year B.Sc. student in Software Engineering at the University of Tehran and a research assistant working with Prof. Ahmad Khonsari (University of Tehran) and Dr. Mahdi Dolati (Sharif University of Technology) on machine learning systems.

My research is about making distributed deep learning training efficient on shared infrastructure. When many training jobs run on the same cluster, they compete for network bandwidth, GPU compute and storage I/O at the same time. I study how to schedule and place these jobs jointly, using mathematical optimization (MILP) and network simulation, so that the whole cluster trains faster.

I also have hands-on experience building distributed systems, with projects using PyTorch, CUDA, Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, HDFS and SLURM.

I am looking for positions. I am interested in research assistant, research master's and PhD positions in distributed ML systems, data center scheduling and network optimization. If you think I could be a good fit for your group, please get in touch.

Publications

  1. Compute and IO-Aware Distributed Training Management in Converged Networks

    Seyyed Ali Aghamiri, Alireza Karimi, Ahmad Khonsari, and Mahdi Dolati

    Submitted IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing (TCC), October 2026

    Jointly optimizes how multiple distributed deep learning jobs share a converged network, accounting for competing communication, computation and I/O demands. The problem is formulated as a Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP) and evaluated with the FlexNet and FlexNetPacket simulators from TopoOpt.
